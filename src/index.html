Performance Mode
Turns off animations and effects and stops the 3D model from auto-rotating. Best for low-end PCs.
Close launcher when the game starts
Frees up memory while you play. Op Launcher closes as soon as Minecraft has started. Not used for profiles with a custom skin: the launcher has to stay open to show it in game.
Show activity on Discord
Shows "Playing Minecraft" with your version and username on your Discord profile while you play. The launcher has to stay open for this to show.
Show my name tag
Shows your own name above your head when you look at yourself in third person (F5). Needs the Op Client mod, which Op Launcher adds to your profile.
